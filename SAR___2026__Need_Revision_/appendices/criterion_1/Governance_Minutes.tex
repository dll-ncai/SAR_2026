\chapter{Departmental and School Governance Minutes and Policy Documents (2026)}\label{app:governance_minutes}

This appendix reproduces the governance minutes and supporting policy documents cited in Criterion~1. These comprise: the Software Engineering Departmental Board of Studies (DBS) meeting at which the revised (2026) Alumni and Employer Survey instruments were reviewed and approved, the subsequent SEECS HoDs' forum and Faculty Board of Studies (FBS), the DBS-approved working paper introducing ``Introduction to Quantum Computing'' as a new elective course, in response to IAB feedback, and the departmental Internship Assessment Policy governing the evaluation of student internships. It also contains the evidence of minor in Software Engineering offerings. Each item is presented as a separate section, with the source PDF embedded in full immediately below its heading.
