\section{University Linkages}

The Software Engineering Department at SEECS maintains strong and sustained engagement with industry through active involvement of its faculty members in consultancy, design supervision, and applied research activities. Faculty members regularly collaborate with industrial organizations by undertaking consultancy assignments and providing expert guidance in their respective domains of specialization. These engagements include on-site visits to industry where faculty contribute to the identification, formulation, and execution of applied research and design projects that address real-world challenges faced by the sector. In addition to offering expert opinions, faculty members also supervise ongoing industrial projects, ensuring that proposed solutions are technically sound, innovative, and aligned with current industry practices and societal needs. Such interactions not only strengthen academia-industry linkage but also facilitate the transfer of knowledge and technology, promote problem-driven research, and enhance the relevance of academic activities to national and industrial priorities. Furthermore, these collaborative efforts contribute significantly to the professional development of faculty and provide valuable exposure to students involved in such projects, thereby supporting the attainment of program educational objectives (PEOs) in line with accreditation requirements. \section{Career Development Centre (CDC)}

NUST supports students in career planning, professional development, internship opportunities, and transition to employment through relevant school-level activities and NUST career services. The Career Development Centre (CDC), listed among the resources and offices associated with NUST's Centre for Innovation and Entrepreneurship, provides a framework for career-related guidance and engagement. These activities complement the university-level placement and employer-engagement services offered through the NUST Placement Office (NPO). Career development support may include the following services, subject to availability through the relevant office or activity:

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt]
    \item Career guidance and professional development;
    \item Curriculum vitae (CV), resume, and cover-letter preparation;
    \item Interview preparation and job-search guidance;
    \item Information about internship and employment opportunities;
    \item Career fairs, employer presentations, and recruitment activities;
    \item Opportunities for students to interact with employers and professionals.
\end{itemize}

Career fairs and employer-engagement activities provide opportunities for students to learn about industry expectations, explore career pathways, and interact with prospective employers. Such activities may also facilitate recruitment and internship applications. Student profiles and Final Year Design Project (FYDP) information can be used to showcase graduate capabilities and student work to interested organisations, where supported by the arrangements for a particular event. SEECS also disseminates relevant employment and internship opportunities through its institutional channels. The school maintains dedicated online pages for career-related announcements and internship information. These resources help students identify opportunities relevant to their academic background and professional interests. Career-related activities contribute to graduate employability by connecting students with employers and encouraging the development of technical, communication, and professional skills. Evidence of specific career fairs, participating employers, recruitment outcomes, and student placements should be maintained in the relevant event records.


\section{Conclusion}

The Software Engineering Department at SEECS has established a comprehensive and sustainable framework for fostering strong industry-academia linkages that effectively support academic excellence, research innovation, and graduate employability. Through an active Industrial Advisory Board, a dedicated Industrial Liaison Office, structured internship programs, and continuous industry feedback mechanisms, the department ensures that its academic programs remain aligned with evolving industrial requirements and professional standards.

The department’s extensive collaborations with national and international industries, research organizations, and technology companies provide valuable opportunities for applied research, consultancy, innovation, and commercialization. Jointly supervised design projects, faculty consultancy engagements, and industry-sponsored research initiatives further strengthen the integration of theoretical knowledge with real-world applications, enabling students to acquire practical skills and professional competencies.

Moreover, the implementation of a documented Continuous Quality Improvement (CQI) process ensures that industrial feedback is systematically analyzed and incorporated into curriculum enhancement, Program Educational Objectives (PEOs) assessment, and strategic planning. These efforts demonstrate the department’s commitment to maintaining relevance, quality, and excellence in software engineering education.

Overall, the strong and diverse industrial linkages maintained by SEECS significantly contribute to student development, research impact, technological innovation, and national socio-economic growth, while ensuring compliance with PEC accreditation requirements and international best practices.
